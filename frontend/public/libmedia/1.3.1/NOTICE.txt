The unmodified player JavaScript is copied from @libmedia/avplayer 1.3.1
during the frontend build. The Wasm decoders, resampler and pitch stretcher
are unmodified upstream assets from the v1.3.1 source revision:
https://github.com/zhaohappy/libmedia/tree/152f629d3021fd8013efa464fcb7b55f9fbe7753

Source, build instructions, and the FFmpeg / SoundTouch sources used to
build these modules are available in that repository, including its lib/
submodules. wasm-manifest.json records the SHA-256 of each shipped module.
No video encoder modules are included.
